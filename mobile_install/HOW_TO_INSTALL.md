# Hakai Protocol — Mobile Install Guide

## Android (Chrome)
1. Open Chrome on your phone
2. Go to: https://jeevan-0508.github.io/hakai-protocol-v2
3. Tap the **three dots menu** (⋮) top right
4. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"**
5. Tap **Install** on the prompt
6. Done — Hakai Protocol icon appears on your home screen

## iOS (Safari)
1. Open Safari on your iPhone/iPad
2. Go to: https://jeevan-0508.github.io/hakai-protocol-v2
3. Tap the **Share button** (□↑) at the bottom
4. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
5. Tap **Add** top right
6. Done — icon appears on your home screen

## Android (Download APK via PWABuilder)
For a proper APK file:
1. Go to https://www.pwabuilder.com
2. Enter: https://jeevan-0508.github.io/hakai-protocol-v2
3. Click "Package for stores"
4. Choose Android → Download APK
5. Install the APK on your phone

## Notes
- The app works offline after first visit (PWA + Service Worker)
- Your progress saves in the app (localStorage per device)
- App icon: indigo H shield
